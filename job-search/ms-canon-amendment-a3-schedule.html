Canon Amendment A3

Delivery cadence rebuilt around a paid position, using a split that already exists in the protocol: fasting is a per-service requirement, not a per-session one.

Drafted 14 Sep 2026 Status PROVISIONAL Amends canon v4 §2, §4 Governs over A1, A2 on cadence only

A3.0 — Why this exists

The premise changed

Canon v4 assumes Metabolic Suite is the primary weekday commitment. It isn't going to be. A paid position has to sit alongside it, and the family is on Medical Assistance, so income has to arrive on a schedule rather than in bursts.

Canon's locked cadence — three mornings, Tue/Thu/Sat — makes that impossible, because it occupies the three most employable blocks in the week. But it occupies them for a reason that only applies to two of the three services.

A3.1 — The load-bearing change

Pre-test state is per-service, not per-session

RMR and Full Suite require the fasted protocol. That is a real physiological constraint and it locks those services to the morning. Nothing here touches it.

VO₂-only carries no fasting requirement. You do not want a fasted subject going to volitional exhaustion — a fed state is the correct pre-test condition for a maximal ramp, not a compromise on one. Canon has been treating the whole operation as though it inherits RMR's constraint, and it never did.

Canon §2 should carry this as an explicit lock, because the entire schedule below rests on it and it will otherwise get re-litigated by whoever reads the RMR protocol first.

A3.4 — What this fixes for free

Three open items partially close

The venue collision (§14, structural). Evening and Saturday use means the clinic is closed. No patients in the waiting room, no cycling kit beside naturopathic patients, no overlap with Haley's schedule. The brand collision you flagged is largely an artifact of operating during clinic hours — outside them it mostly dissolves.

The sublease (gate 5, no movement since Aug 10). A Saturday-morning and two-evening footprint is a materially smaller ask than three weekday mornings. Cheaper at FMV, easier to paper, and an easier conversation to open.

Report turnaround. The old cadence left Friday and Monday to write reports. This one frees every weekday morning — roughly 17 hours a week for reports, the VO₂ block library that doesn't exist yet, and demand work. Canon's "within three business days" gets easier to hold, not harder.

A3.5 — Hard gate

⛔ Evening solo maximal testing — do not start on this

This amendment moves the maximal-effort test to weekday evenings, alone, in a closed building. Canon §14 already carries solo maximal testing as one of three structural risks, and the carrier question sits unanswered in the Reily draft that has not been sent since Sept 3.

The schedule change makes that risk worse, not neutral. It raises three existing open items from "before Oct 15" to "before the first evening VO₂":

  • Reily's answer on solo maximal testing — and whether an evening, single-operator pattern changes the carrier's position at all
  • The AED actually purchased — gates sweep shows no evidence of an order; canon §5 has the correct $1,194 set
  • EAP reconciled with the termination criteria, and SC-1's "physician clearance" squared against PAR-Q+'s "medical clearance"

Until all three close: run VO₂ in the Saturday block alongside the fasted services, not in the evening. Capacity is lower and the schedule is tighter, but nothing is uninsured and nobody is alone on a bike at 8pm. The evening blocks open when the gates close.

A3.6 — Canon and artifacts this changes

  1. §2 delivery row — a deliberate reopening of a LOCKED decision

    Replace the Tue/Thu/Sat morning cadence with the block structure in A3.2. Record it as a reopening with its reason, so it doesn't read later as drift.

  2. §2 — add the pre-test state lock

    State per-service: RMR and Full Suite fasted, morning-bound. VO₂-only has no fasting requirement and may be scheduled at any hour. This is the sentence the whole amendment rests on.

  3. §4 capacity table — rebuild

    The runbook already found the existing table wrong (the "2 × Full Suite + 1 × RMR … $675 morning" does not exist). It has to be rewritten regardless; write it to A3.3 rather than to the old cadence.

  4. §2 report delivery — recompute the promise

    "Within three business days" still holds, but the processing blocks move: Saturday tests land Wednesday, Tuesday evening lands Friday, Thursday evening lands Tuesday. Weekday mornings are the writing time.

  5. MS_ACUITY_BUILD_v1 — rebuild before standing up the account

    It is configured for Tue/Thu/Sat with a 240-minute cap and four appointment types, and it carries 22 TODOs. The account does not exist yet, so this change is nearly free today and expensive in three weeks. Do it before the build, not after.

  6. Website v1.1 — fix before deploying

    The zip says three mornings. It is not on GitHub or Cloudflare yet. Same argument: change it while nothing is live.
