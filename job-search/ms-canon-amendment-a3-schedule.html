Canon Amendment A3

Delivery cadence rebuilt around a minimized clinical schedule rather than an outside job — using a split already in the protocol: fasting is a per-service requirement, not a per-session one.

Rev 2 · 14 Sep 2026 Status PROVISIONAL Amends canon v4 §2, §4 Supersedes A3 rev 1

A3.0 — Premise

It was never a capacity problem

Rev 1 assumed an outside job had to be absorbed. That's withdrawn. Metabolic Suite is the additional income, PNH shrinks to its real clinical load, and the constraint is a school calendar.

The arithmetic that drove this: PNH holds 19 hours a week to deliver about 4. Tuesday 9–5, Thursday 9–5, Saturday 10–1, against one new patient at two hours and one to two follow-ups at an hour each. Roughly 15 hours a week are committed and empty — across exactly the three windows Metabolic Suite needs most.

Nothing here cuts Metabolic Suite. It moves four hours of care out of fifteen hours of prime schedule.

A3.1 — The load-bearing change

Pre-test state is per-service, not per-session

RMR and Full Suite require the fasted protocol. Real physiological constraint, morning-bound. Untouched.

VO₂-only carries no fasting requirement. A fed state is the correct pre-test condition for a maximal ramp, not a compromise on one. Canon has been applying RMR's constraint to the whole operation, and it never applied.

Canon §2 should carry this as an explicit lock, or it gets re-litigated by whoever reads the RMR protocol first.

A3.5 — School dependencies

What the calendar actually imposes

⚠ Launch day is a school holiday

Thursday October 15 is Education MN / MEA break — K-12 closed district-wide. Your first paying client is booked into it. Club Care runs an Optional Day (7:00a–5:30p) but it is separate registration at only one or two sites, not automatic.

MEA weekend is also one of Minnesota's heaviest family travel weekends, which is worth weighing before anchoring a launch to it. Nobody holds a reservation against the 15th yet.

Elementary bells are staggered three ways — 7:45/2:15 (Carver, Chanhassen, Clover Ridge), 8:30/3:00 (Bluff Creek, Jonathan), 9:30/4:00 (La Academia, Victoria, Kinder Academy). A 1h45m spread. If you do drop-off yourself, the tier decides whether a weekday fasted block is worth running at all: at a 9:30 bell, first test slips to ~10:15 and a Full Suite eats until nearly 1:00.

Club Care AM opens 6:30 and makes the tier irrelevant. That is the unlock for Tue/Thu mornings. Cost is $15/day at most sites ($12 Clover Ridge, $20 La Academia/Victoria), but there is a three-day-per-week minimum — so a two-morning need buys three days, roughly $45/week.

Against $500 per fasted morning that is trivially worth it — if you can get in. Enrollment is capped, priority registration closed in April, and at least one site was already waitlisted. Verify availability before the grid depends on it.

No recurring early-release or late-start day exists, so there is no weekly disruption to design around. Across the year, seven Tue/Thu fall on no-school days; Club Care Optional Days cover Oct 15 and the March 22–25 spring break, but Tuesday January 19 is closed with no coverage.
